## Platform

web

## Users

Marketing leads at small and mid-sized companies in Germany and Europe: marketing managers, owners who run their own marketing, and the person in a company of 10 to 200 people who "does the marketing" next to other jobs. They arrive drowning in repetitive campaign, lead and reporting work, usually with a stack of tools that do not talk to each other, and they want that work to run by itself inside the tools they already pay for. They read German or English; the site serves both.

## Product Purpose

addmagic designs, builds and runs AI-powered marketing automations: lead nurturing, campaign workflows, creative operations and reporting intelligence. The company exists so that a small marketing team gets its repetitive work done by systems and keeps its time for ideas and judgement. Success for a client is a flow that runs every day without them touching it, that they can read on one page, and that still has a human deciding where it matters.

## Positioning

AI engineering combined with real marketing and creative craft. The founder comes from agency-side marketing work (campaign planning, content, social media, brand sites), so the automations are built by someone who has done the manual version of the job, not by a tool vendor. Everything is built inside the client's existing CRM, ad, content and analytics tools; every flow has a human review step and a one-page plain-language description. Tagline: "Less manual. More magic." (German: "Weniger Handarbeit. Mehr Magie.")

## Operating Context

Work runs in four phases: Discover (map the client's tools, handovers and time sinks, half a day), Design (draft each flow: triggers, human decision points, output), Build (connect tools, tune prompts to the client's voice, test with real data), Run (monthly measurement and improvement). Tools the company genuinely works with: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Shopify, WordPress, Mailchimp, Brevo, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, n8n, OpenAI, Claude, Figma, Canva, Semrush. First contact is by email; there is no booking tool or form backend yet.

## Product Principles

1. Humans decide where it matters: offers, tone and spend always pass a review step.
2. Build inside the client's tools; never ask them to migrate or learn a new platform.
3. Plain language: every flow is explained on one page the whole team can read.
4. Show the mechanism, not the hype: demonstrate how a flow runs rather than claiming results.
5. Marketing craft first, automation second: the output must read like good marketing, not like a bot wrote it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast checked on both themes, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable controls, reduced motion respected, semantic HTML. Bilingual content must be complete in both languages, not partially translated.
