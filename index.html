How it works

From first call to running system in four phases.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map your tools, your handovers and the places where time is lost. Half a day, no slides.

    Output: a map of your marketing operations
  2. 02

    Design

    We draft the flows with you: what triggers what, where a human decides, what the result looks like.

    Output: one page per flow, readable by everyone
  3. 03

    Build

    We connect the tools, tune the prompts to your voice and test with real data before anything goes live.

    Output: tested automations inside your systems
  4. 04

    Run

    We keep it running, measure it and improve it month by month. You stay in control of every step.

    Output: a system that gets better with use
